# docs/

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `plans/` | The project brief (`LeoRodrigues_ProjectProposal_GenRec_MechInterpAnalysis.pdf`, RQ1–RQ4, Experiments 1–6), `RESEARCH_PLAN.md` (what is established and the roadmap), and `THREE_HOUR_EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md` (the experiment ladder and its interpretation constraints). `DIFFUSION_INTERVENTIONS.md` (DiffGRM intervention ideas from the literature) `DIFFUSION_STEP_PATCHING_PLAN.md` (the approved 2026-10-13 plan for step-level patching and the reveal trace) `DIFFUSION_MECHANISMS_PLAN.md` (2026-10-14: encoder features, paired-run patching, step switch and decode perturbations on all 20 checkpoints) and `COPYING_GRID_PLAN.md` (2026-10-21: every experiment and four additions on the coauthor's OneDiffRec grid; runbook `clusters/GRID_RUNBOOK.md`). |
| `results/semantic_mapping/` | `SEMANTIC_MAPPING_RESULTS.md` (commands and outputs), `SEMANTIC_MAPPING_TAKEAWAYS.md` (findings and figure captions), `figures/`, the offline `semantic_explorer.html`, and `rqkmeans_prefix14.xlsx` (the worked prefix-14 example). |
| `results/final_maps/` | `sidlens_final_maps_paper.html`: the paper-facing page (every map result the WWW'27 draft displays, its old and v3 values, what to change, figures and the collision-cause table, old against v3). `sidlens_final_maps.html`: the self-contained report of structure exp4 (the final-run v3 SID maps of both catalogs; figures embedded), `facts.md` (every number it cites, with its source table) and `figures/` (PDF/PNG/CSV), all written by `experiments/structure/exp4_final_maps/report.py`. |
| `results/diffusion_mechanisms/` | `index.html`: the self-contained report on how DiffGRM arrives at a recommendation (X1–X4 of `plans/DIFFUSION_MECHANISMS_PLAN.md` with controlled exp1, exp2, exp7, exp8 and the reveal trace), built by `python -m sidlens.viz.diffusion_report` from the summary CSVs. |
| `results/mechanisms_review/` | The review copy of every mechanistic experiment (15 AR, 10 DiffGRM, 2 SID-map) with a storyline tab (RQs, claims, the experiments behind each, write-up order, next steps) and a "Final important experiments" tab (the experiments to run on the final-run checkpoints: shared steps, then codebooks/collisions, Qwen-AR and DiffGRM, main before supplementary, with coverage of every earlier experiment). `build_report.py` reads the summaries under `$SIDLENS_WORK/derived` and writes the self-contained `sidlens_mechanisms_review.html`; the text is in `content_{ar,diffusion,maps,story,final}.py`. Provisional until the final-run checkpoints are analysed. |
| `results/diffusion_trace/` | The review page for the DiffGRM reveal trace (representation/exp4): `build_report.py` reads the run's result files and writes `sidlens_diffusion_trace.html` and a `_standalone.html` copy that opens from disk. |
| `clusters/` | `GRID_RUNBOOK.md` (the coauthor's steps for running every experiment on the OneDiffRec grid and sending `results_remote/` back), `CROSS_CLUSTER.md` (running SidLens on another cluster; bundles through private HF dataset repos; activation sizes), `AR_CLUSTER_PROMPT.md` (prompt for the AR capture and intervention agent there), and `MAIN_PROJECT_DATA_PROMPT.md` (the upstream data-extraction prompt). |
| `paper/` | WWW'27 paper: the current draft (`rewrite-20261004/`, with `RQ_PLAN.md`), the writing guides (`WRITING.md`, `REWRITING.md`), and `archive/` (earlier zips, PDFs and rewrite folders, indexed in its `README.md`). |

Controlled-experiment write-ups stay beside their code, in
`experiments/controlled/*/RESULTS.md`. Bulk outputs live under
`$SIDLENS_WORK/derived/`, not here.
